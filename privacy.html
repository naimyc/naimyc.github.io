LangStudio

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

LangStudio ("the app") is made by naimyc, the developer named on its Google Play page, based in Germany. This policy says what the app collects, why, and who else sees it. Contact: naimyachou@yahoo.es.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account: your email address and the name you choose. Used to sign you in (we send one email to confirm the address, and one if you ask to reset your password), to keep your words on all your devices, and to show your name to the learners you chat with.
  • What you learn: the words and boxes you save, your progress, your streak and weekly promise. Used to run the app and to sync it.
  • Chat: your messages, the corrections you make and receive, and your learner profile (the languages you learn and speak, the character you chose, when you were last online). Seen by the learners you chat with. Boxes you choose to share are seen by other learners.
  • Reports and blocks: when you report or block someone, we keep who, which message and the reason you gave, to keep the chat safe.
  • Notifications: a push token for your device, to tell you about new messages. You can turn them off under You → Notifications.
  • Ads: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, information about your device, and how you interact with ads. In the EU, UK and similar regions you are asked for consent first, and can change your choice under You → Ad privacy. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to LangStudio Plus, Google Play handles the payment. We learn only that you have an active subscription.
  • Translation and dictionary: words and sentences you translate go through our server to Microsoft's translation service (Azure AI Translator, in the EU), and the words you look up go to Wiktionary (through Kaikki), to get their meaning. Translations are kept in a shared cache on our server, so the same text is not sent twice. The cache records the text and its translation, never who asked.
  • On your device only: what you say into the microphone (speaking practice) and pictures or PDFs you scan for words are processed on the device and not sent to us.

We do not sell your data, and we use it only to run the app.

Who processes it

  • Appwrite (hosting, servers in Frankfurt, Germany): your account, what you learn, chat.
  • Google: Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), AdMob (ads), Google Play (purchases).
  • Microsoft (Azure AI Translator, servers in the EU): translation.
  • Wiktionary / Kaikki: dictionary look-ups.

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete your account and all its data at any time, in the app or by email. See Delete your account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected, have it deleted, limit or object to its use, and take it with you. Write to naimyachou@yahoo.es; we answer within a month. We use your data to provide the app you signed up for (your account, learning, chat), with your consent for personalised ads, and in our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe (reports). You can also complain to a data protection authority, in your country or in Germany.

Children

LangStudio is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your data on the server is limited to your own account, apart from what you choose to share.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
