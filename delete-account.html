Delete your LangStudio account

LangStudio, by naimyc

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open LangStudio and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Scroll to the end and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete account.

Your account and its data are deleted at once, on our servers and on the device.

Without the app

Email naimyachou@yahoo.es from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my LangStudio account. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, name, password).
  • Your words, boxes, progress, roads, streak and weekly promise.
  • Your learner profile and character.
  • Your conversations, with every message, correction and game in them, for you and for the learners you talked to.
  • Your block list.

What is kept

  • Reports you made about other learners are kept as moderation records, without your account, only as long as needed to keep the app safe.
  • Purchases are recorded by Google Play, and ad data by Google AdMob, under Google's own policies. Cancel a LangStudio Plus subscription in the Play Store (Payments & subscriptions): deleting the account does not cancel it.
